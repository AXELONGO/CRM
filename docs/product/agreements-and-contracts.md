# Agreements and Contracts

Agreements are created from accepted quote versions. Drafts are editable; issued versions are immutable. The MVP supports fixed project, hourly consulting, retainer, signable proposal and statement of work documents.

The flow is: accepted quote, draft, optional internal review, issue, envelope, recipient acceptance or external PDF review, evidence bundle and executed state. Payment schedules are informational only. Projects, invoices and payments are outside this module.

# Pilot Support and Training

Support hours must be stated honestly by the single operator; no 24/7 promise. Use one approved channel and never request passwords, tokens, card data, QR codes or raw exports.

- P0: suspected cross-tenant leak, incorrect mass payment or critical secret. Stop activity, preserve evidence, notify owner immediately.
- P1: unavailable service or critical data flow. Acknowledge during support hours within 30 minutes; use recovery runbook.
- P2: degraded feature with workaround. Track and schedule.
- P3: minor defect or feedback. Record separately from incidents.

Training guides: admin handles members/brand/settings; sales handles pipeline/quotes; execution handles projects/tasks/files; finance handles receivables/reconciliation; clients use only portal flows; operator handles health, jobs, backups and waits.

Capture feedback as context, impact, frequency, evidence, workaround, priority, decision and target release. Do not build one-off requests without a repeated pattern.
